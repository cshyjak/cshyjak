## How I think about AI and software
 
From my Prairie Dev Con 2026 keynote, [*Generation Is Cheap. Judgment Isn't.*](https://github.com/cshyjak/talks):
 
- **Generation is cheap. Ownership is not.** The risk isn't AI writing bad code. It's AI writing plausible code that nobody understands well enough to own.
- **Review is the new bottleneck.** Output scales faster than trust, so review intent and assumptions, not just diffs.
- **Feeling faster isn't being faster.** Measure outcomes, and keep the reps alive: read code, debug, trace failures, decide what not to build.
- **Trust boundaries should be explicit.** The question isn't "can the AI do this?" but "who owns the risk when it's wrong?" Make ground truth, blast radius and sign-off visible.
- **Observability should answer questions.** Tie signals to an owner and an action, and instrument decisions, not only infrastructure.
- **Not anti-AI. Not all-in. Intentional.**
